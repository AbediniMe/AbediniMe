## Engineering & Research Principles

- **Evidence before assumptions** — make questions, evidence, constraints, and trade-offs explicit.
- **Reproducibility and clarity** — document methods, dependencies, setup, and limitations where applicable.
- **Clear architecture** — keep components, interfaces, and system boundaries understandable.
- **Build, evaluate, refine** — turn ideas into testable implementations and improve them iteratively.
- **Responsible biomedical engineering** — account for safety, validation, privacy, biocompatibility, and clinical evidence when relevant.

## Projects & Technical Portfolio

This profile is intended to present practical work alongside interdisciplinary research and technical exploration. As projects are published, their repositories should make it easy to understand:

- **Problem and motivation** — what question the work addresses and why it matters
- **Scope and approach** — what is included, key assumptions, and how the work is structured
- **Implementation and use** — technologies, setup instructions, examples, and expected outputs, where applicable
- **Evaluation and limitations** — available evidence, current status, known constraints, and next steps

I will distinguish prototypes, ongoing investigations, and validated results rather than presenting them as equivalent. Please assess each project from its own code, documentation, and evidence.
